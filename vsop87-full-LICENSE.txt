VSOP87 full analytical provider — provenance

The coefficient arrays in vsop87-full.js are copied without numerical changes
from astronomia (six full VSOP87B data modules), under its MIT license below.
The UMD wrapper, vector conversion, and analytical derivative evaluator were
added for Bharat Ephemeris. There are no runtime dependencies or downloads.

Upstream revision: 71e16c942b143a1a5a067b6aab2d8532064704ae
Source: https://github.com/commenthol/astronomia
Theory: P. Bretagnon & G. Francou (1988), Astronomy and Astrophysics 202, 309.
Original series catalogue: https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/VI/81
Original frame/time documentation (BDL-9502 mirror):
https://github.com/ctdk/vsop87/blob/master/vsop87.txt

The theory's original integration constants were adjusted to JPL DE200.
This provider evaluates analytical series; it does not use DE440, Swiss
Ephemeris, downloaded numerical ephemerides, or fitted residual corrections.

Input is Julian date TT; output is geometric heliocentric position and velocity,
AU and AU per TT day, in fixed equatorial FK5 J2000 axes. Light time, aberration,
gravitational light deflection, precession, nutation, and topocentric reduction
are intentionally the responsibility of the caller. FK5 is not labeled ICRF.
