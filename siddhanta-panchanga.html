१ · स्थान · दिन — place and day

The position stays on this device: the page sends nothing anywhere (your browser finds the position itself). The UTC offset is fixed; enter a summer-time offset yourself.

मास-गणना · month reckoning

Astronomical years: 0 = 1 BCE, −1 = 2 BCE. The two Sūrya-Siddhānta choices take any day from −50,000 to +50,000; the dṛk choice is served for 1850–2150 only and says so outside it.

उन्नत · advanced: the Ujjayinī meridian

The text counts days from midnight on the Laṅkā–Ujjayinī meridian. 75.7885° E is the modern position of Ujjain (the value the rest of this product uses), not a figure from the text; change it here if you hold another. A place's deśāntara is its longitude less this.

Clock times are your UTC offset's. In the two Sūrya-Siddhānta choices an instant is the text's count of days from midnight at Laṅkā, so a clock time is that count less the meridian's longitude (÷ 360 of a day) plus your offset, and no other correction enters; the dṛk choice gives universal time from its own series. Beside each time is its count from sunrise in ghaṭī (घ) and vināḍī (वि): of the star-wheel's turn (SS 1.11-1.12) in the text's choices, of the civil day in the dṛk choice.

४ · मास — the month, day by day

Each day shows the tithi and nakṣatra at its sunrise. A tithi that begins and ends between two sunrises is marked kṣaya; one that runs through two sunrises, vṛddhi. Choose a day to see its pañcāṅga.

५ · वर्ष — the year: its months, adhika and kṣaya

The year numbered Y here is the lunisolar year of Kali Y + 3101 (years gone at its start), which in our era begins in March or April of Y. Any year from −50,000 to +50,000 in the two Sūrya-Siddhānta choices; 1850–2149 in the dṛk choice.

८ · विंशोत्तरी दशा — Vimśottarī daśā

Only the birth instant matters: the Moon's nakṣatra then, in the choice above. The birth date is read in the calendar chosen in section १. The place gives the clock's UTC offset (enter the offset in force at the birth, summer time included); its latitude and longitude are not used. Birth details are not stored. In the dṛk choice the year and the balance are the tier's own (shown with the table).

९ · परिचय — about these numbers

  • सूर्य-सिद्धान्त + परमेश्वर-संस्कार · Sūrya-Siddhānta + Parameśvara's saṃskāra (the default): the text's own model, with the mean places that the paramparā's record names moved by its arcminutes. The record is read on this device from corpus/parampara/samskara.json: it was fitted to the eclipses Parameśvara recorded. Every block below names this choice and its rules.
  • सूर्य-सिद्धान्त · Sūrya-Siddhānta: the text exactly as it stands — its revolutions, true Sun and Moon (chapter 2, with Mādhava's sine), ayanāṃśa (3.9-3.10) and eclipse rules (chapters 4-6).
  • आधुनिक भारतीय (दृक्) · Modern Bhāratīya (dṛk): the owner's own series, served for 1850–2150 and refused outside it. Its engine, span and the agreement measured in this repository's tests are printed in each block's label as the engine states them; nothing else is computed in the browser to compare it with.
  • Your own observations are optional. The default choice already carries the saṃskāra from the paramparā's recorded observations; if you wish to keep your own beside the text, the observation companion वेध · vedha does that, and nothing you record there changes a value on this page.
  • Printed pañcāṅgas use other models and other ayanāṃśas, so a tithi's end can differ by hours, and a festival or a saṅkrānti can fall a day apart.
  • Festival rules (which tithi, in which part of the day, which of two days) are simplified common practice and are marked unverified, unless a row is marked as from the text: there is no Rohiṇī check for Janmāṣṭamī and no daśamī-vedha for Ekādaśī. The names of most yogas, of the movable karaṇas after Bava and of the tithis are the standard lists; no text in this repository states them.

मान्यताएँ · conventions

  • Months are amānta (new moon to new moon), named by the saṅkrānti in them, with adhika and kṣaya months; the rule is printed in each block's label. The pūrṇimānta name is derived on this page — the dark half takes the next month's name, and an adhika month stays adhika — and is labelled common practice.
  • The karaṇas around amāvāsyā follow SS 2.67's order in the two Sūrya-Siddhānta choices (Śakuni, Nāga, Catuṣpada, Kiṃstughna); the dṛk choice uses the common order, labelled as such.
  • The month grid derives kṣaya and vṛddhi tithis by comparing the tithis at two sunrises.
  • The daśā year in the text's choices is the Sūrya-Siddhānta's solar year, 365.2587565 civil days (SS 14.10 with 1.37), and the balance at birth is by the time gone in the birth nakṣatra (BPHS 46.16); the other choices dasha.js offers are in the form.
  • Clock times use 75.7885° E for Ujjain's meridian, its modern position, not a figure from the text.
