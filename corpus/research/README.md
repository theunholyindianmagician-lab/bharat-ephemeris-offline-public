# corpus/research — the research page's data

Two generated files, read by `ganita-shala.html` (गणित-शाला) and precached by `sw.js`; neither is typed by hand.

- **`parampara-apply.json`** — `node scripts/parampara-apply.cjs --write`. Every applicable record of `corpus/parampara/registry.json` applied as a model of `samskara.js` or `parahita-madhyama.js` and scored against the Modern Bhāratīya (dṛk) tier in the tropical frame over 2026 (every 5 days) and 1900–2100 (every 97 days): candidate minus sky, arcminutes (mean, rms, max) for the Sun, the Moon, the elongation and the ayanāṃśa against Citrā-pakṣa. The candidates and what was adopted are named in the file (design doc §24.2–24.3).
- **`derivations.json`** — the derivations ledger: one entry per derivation of the engine with the text it starts from, its method, and the figures its tests assert, each figure copied from the named file and kept only when found there verbatim (or, for a data file, its values found in it); `figuresNotFoundVerbatim` would list any that were not. Thirty-one entries were extracted by one reader per derivation (2026-10-09), five of the same day were written by hand from their tests; `notYetInLedger` lists what the design document describes that no entry covers yet.

- **`parampara-apply.json` → `measured`** — the five forms of the sibling Kerala-corrections file (SSEA / sphuta / web) fitted against the sky as candidates on the Kerala tier, with provenance per form; the joint candidate `kerala+all+bija` (a bīja on the Sun's, Moon's, apogees' and node's means, the Moon's second manda harmonic and reduction, the five planets with bīja, epicycle scales and second harmonic — every number fitted through the tier's own arithmetic, `measured.planets.bodies`); `ss-tier.js measuredFromRecord(record, candidate)` reads a candidate; since 2026-10-12 (owner) `kerala+all+bija` is the Kerala tier's default (`{ measured: false }` gives the bare paramparā), and `parampara-record.js` carries the `measured` block for the browser.
- **`validate-oos.json`** — the Kerala default measured OUT OF SAMPLE (`scripts/validate-oos.cjs --write`): a holdout grid midway between the fit samples (1862–2138), temporal holdouts 1850–1862 and 2138–2150 (the dṛk tier's edges, never fitted), actual tithi-end instants by root-finding on each tier (1850, 2026, 2138), and the eclipses of 1900–2100 (lunar middles and magnitudes, solar visibility at Ujjain), against the dṛk tier; the fit's own grid is never used here.
- **`ssea-audit.json`** — the owner's SSEA engine (uploaded 2026-10-12, 128 files) read file by file by a workflow of readers with three refuters per claimed derivation and a critic; every quote re-checked; this repository's own verified findings; the decisions (what was taken, what was measured, what stays a candidate, what was found wanting).
- **`library-sweep.json`** — ten readers, one per edition, for what each text holds about time structure, number and measurement; every quoted line re-checked at its line number (`verseFound`), the editions' own commentary marked apart from the verse, the readers' measurement candidates with their caveats. 196 findings, 136 found verbatim.

- **`generator-2-search.json`** — the search for any basis for a doubling (generator-2) daśā or transit rule: readers over the ten editions, the repository's research and its code, three refuters per positive claim, every quoted line re-checked, every stated daśā/tārā/transit order tested against x ↦ 2x and x ↦ x + 1; the doubling cycles of ℤ/9 and ℤ/27 from `sukshma-kala.js generatorOrbits`.

All carry their `generatedBy`. A figure on the page is never retyped from memory: it is this data or a live computation.
